🚨 Call tracking must be live before ads go live. Without it, Google Ads has no conversion signal and Smart Bidding optimizes toward clicks, not calls. 90-second minimum duration is your quality filter — this is the single most important configuration decision before launch.
Setup Steps — In Order
1. Create CallRail account + number pool
~30 min
Pool of 4–6 tracking numbers. DNI swaps displayed number by traffic source.
2. Install DNI snippet in Lovable index.html
~15 min
Must go in <head> of index.html root — not just a component. React SPA needs it at root level.
3. Connect CallRail → Google Ads
~20 min
CallRail Settings → Integrations → Google Ads. Passes call conversions back for Smart Bidding.
4. Set 90-second minimum duration threshold
Decision ✓
Import only calls ≥ 90 seconds as conversions in Google Ads. Filters hangups, wrong numbers, misdials. Revisit at Day 30 based on actual duration distribution.
5. Enable call recording + disposition tagging
Phase 2
Tag calls: Quote Presented / Sold / No Answer / Wrong Number / Hangup. Enables value-based bidding later.
Platform + Consent Notes
CallRail
Recommended
~$45–65/mo · Native Google Ads integration · DNI · Call recording · Keyword-level attribution · Right-sized for <$5K/mo budget.
MA + TX Consent
Both states are one-party consent for call recording. Best practice: add brief disclosure ("This call may be recorded for quality purposes") to cover edge cases if calls cross state lines.
Conversion Actions in Google Ads
Primary: Website call via CallRail ≥ 90 sec · drives Smart Bidding
Secondary: Phone call from ad (Google native) · catches SERP call extension clicks

07 — Angle 1 · Fight "Too Expensive" Head-On
26.2% of all leads — the #1 loss reason — cite price as the exit. NCI leads are even more price-resistant (29%). Creative and lander copy should preempt this with specific savings claims anchored to real data: "MA drivers pay an avg of $2,241/yr — here's how we compare." CI leads who churn to competitors need urgency and social proof.
Angle 2 · Age 18–25 Is Untapped High-Value
This cohort pays the highest average premiums ($2,847) but has the worst bind rate (23.8%). They over-index on "Too Expensive" because they have no price anchor. Creative framing auto insurance as a life milestone could meaningfully move conversion here.
Angle 3 · TX Needs Its Own Fix
TX has 149 leads but only 17.4% bind rate — less than half of MA's 32.9%. This is a carrier fit problem, a handling problem, or both. Audit TX-bound leads vs TX-bound policies to find where they're falling off.
Angle 4 · Expand to NJ, NH, CT (Data Supports It)
NJ (66.7% bind), NH (50.0%), and ME (47.1%) all outperform MA on bind rate. The Northeast carrier network appears to work well outside MA. The GTM already recommends CT and NJ as post-launch expansions; this data validates the call.
Angle 5 · "Shopping" Leads Are Not Dead
301 leads (10.2%) left with disposition "Shopping" — they got a quote, didn't bind, and are comparing. A re-engagement sequence (email/text 48–72hrs later: "Did you find a better rate? We'd like to match it") at 20% recovery = ~60 additional binds.
Angle 6 · 248 "Sale In Process" Leads Need Pursuit
248 leads are stuck in "Sale In Process" — not yet resolved. At the historical 30.5% bind rate, this pipeline should yield ~75 additional policies. Each CI completion = $50, each NCI = $25 under the new quote model, on top of eventual commission.
