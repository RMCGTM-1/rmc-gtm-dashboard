Revenue Impact Model

At a $424 CPL and 1,000 paid sessions: MLP delivers ~150 form completions at a cost-per-completion of $2,827 per 1,000 visits. RAISE base case delivers ~280 completions for the same spend — a cost-per-completion of $1,514. That's approximately $1,300 in recovered media value per 1,000 visitors, compounding with every dollar of Meta or Google spend behind the form. At $10K/month in paid traffic, RAISE base case returns roughly $13K in additional lead value monthly, purely from form optimization — with no increase in ad spend.

Important Caveat

These are projections, not guarantees. Actual results depend on traffic quality (paid Meta audiences tend to be lower intent than paid search), the offer (RaiseMyCoverage's brand positioning vs. generic aggregators), Bindable API reliability, and whether retargeting infrastructure is in place to recover form abandoners. The model also assumes the form is deployed on the actual raisemycoverage.com domain — performance will degrade if there's still a redirect to a third-party domain.

08 — Priority Actions

What to Build, Test, and Measure First

Must-Do Before Launch
  • Deploy form on raisemycoverage.com domain — no redirect to MLP. Every redirect loses ~5–8% of traffic to domain distrust.
  • Verify Bindable API integration on the review/submit step. If the POST fails silently, you lose every lead.
  • Add real star rating and review count to step 1 trust bar. Must be real (4.8★ · 2,400+ reviews), not placeholder copy.
  • Set up step-level event tracking in GA4 or Mixpanel. You cannot optimize what you cannot measure.
  • Test all 13 steps on iOS Safari and Android Chrome — the two dominant mobile browsers for your target audience.
First 30-Day A/B Tests
  • Test coverage goal step (step 2) vs. going directly to PII. Even a small intent frame likely improves PII completion 8–12%.
  • Test progress bar starting at 8% vs. 0% on step 1. The Endowed Progress Effect should lift step 1→2 transition by 5–10%.
  • Test "No spam, no calls" explicit language below email field vs. current copy. Addresses the #1 abandonment cause at PII.
  • Test date shortcut buttons (Today / In 3 days) on policy start vs. calendar-only. Should remove ~1.5 min from completion time.
Phase 2 Improvements (60–90 Days)
  • Add inline field validation on email and phone. 11–13% conversion lift from real-time pass/fail feedback on blur.
  • Build partial-submit abandonment recovery. If user completes steps 1–3, capture email and trigger a re-engagement flow.
  • Add "Insurance card scan" option on vehicle step — inspired by Jerry. Eliminates VIN lookup friction for ~40% of users who have their card accessible.
  • Implement save-and-resume session tokens. Users interrupted mid-form should be able to continue, not restart.
Longer-Term Optimizations
  • Personalize step 2 copy based on Meta audience segment. A 22-year-old first-time buyer and a 45-year-old switching carriers have different triggers — the form should speak to both.
  • Add live agent chat widget at coverage selection and review steps. Insurance phone leads convert 10–15x more revenue than web-only leads (Invoca, 2024).
  • Consider adding a "What will my rate be?" micro-estimate before the full form. Even a rough range ("Drivers in your ZIP typically pay $X–$Y/month") dramatically improves form starts.
  • Add The Zebra-style Insurability Score post-quote — gives users a reason to return and engage even if they don't bind immediately.
The Single Biggest Lever

Every analysis points to the same conclusion: the moment you ask for PII determines whether the majority of your paid traffic completes the form or leaves. The MLP form asks for it at step 2. Insurify, The Zebra, and Jerry ask for it mid-form or last. The new RAISE form asks for it at step 3, after two engagement signals (ZIP entry and coverage goal). This one structural change is worth more than any design improvement, copy optimization, or trust badge — and it's already in the form we built. Don't undo it.
